And these are the rules I'd make non-negotiable
Never say:
“hackathon”
“prototype”
“project”
“we built”
“our system”
“our model”

Instead say:

EventReady
EventReady analyzes
EventReady recommends
EventReady compares

That tiny linguistic change makes a surprising difference.

Also don't spend screen time proving that the technology exists. Show the result of the technology.

The judges can see the AI working. What they need to feel is:

“This is polished enough that someone could use it tomorrow.”

And honestly, your controlled colour comparison + one clear winner + real shopping results is a stronger story than trying to cram every AI capability into the video.
