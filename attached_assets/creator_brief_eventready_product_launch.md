# EventReady AI — Product Launch Video

## Overview

- **Target duration:** 2 minutes 20 seconds
- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, uploaded publicly to YouTube
- **Style:** Premium fashion-tech advertisement with a real product demonstration
- **Tone:** Decisive, elegant, intelligent, trustworthy
- **Audience:** Anyone choosing a wedding-guest outfit online
- **Core promise:** EventReady AI does not merely visualize an outfit. It decides which outfit works, explains why, and lets the user verify the decision.
- **Recording mode:** Real Live Mode with real photos and real YouCam results. Do not show Demo Mode.
- **Music:** Modern editorial pulse, no lyrics. Start restrained, lift at the palette reveal, resolve cleanly under the final line.

## Shot-by-Shot Recording Script

### Shot 1 — The interruption

- **Time:** 0:00–0:05
- **Visual:** Rapid montage of three inconsistent AI outfit images, or three browser tabs showing different wedding outfits. Change the image every 0.8 seconds. End on a hard cut to black.
- **On-screen text:** `Seeing isn't deciding.`
- **Voiceover:** “ChatGPT can show you in a saree. It can't tell you which saree.”
- **Edit:** Begin mid-motion. Add a subtle shutter sound on each image change. The message must still make sense with the video muted.

### Shot 2 — Brand reveal

- **Time:** 0:05–0:13
- **Visual:** Fade from black into the EventReady AI landing page. Slowly push toward “Your personal wedding guest stylist,” then move to the primary button.
- **On-screen text:** `Not a filter. A verdict.`
- **Voiceover:** “Because seeing an outfit isn't the same as knowing it works. EventReady AI turns one photo into a clear, explainable wedding-guest decision. Not a filter. A verdict.”
- **Action:** Click **Start my styling** at the end of the shot.

### Shot 3 — Intent that changes the answer

- **Time:** 0:13–0:29
- **Visual:** Record the preferences screen in three clean beats:
  1. Select the desired **Style Vibe**.
  2. Select **Daytime** or **Evening**.
  3. Select the real dressing tradition for the recorded run.
- **On-screen text:** `Your event. Your mood. Your tradition.`
- **Voiceover:** “Start with how you want to show up. Classic or bold. Daylight or evening. Western, Indian, East Asian or Middle Eastern. These aren't decorative questions. Every answer changes what EventReady recommends.”
- **Edit:** Use a small punch-in on each selection. Let each selected state remain visible for about one second.

### Shot 4 — Two photos

- **Time:** 0:29–0:39
- **Visual:** Show the selfie and full-body upload cards. Cut directly from empty cards to completed previews; do not show the operating-system file picker.
- **On-screen text:** `One selfie. One full-body photo.`
- **Voiceover:** “Then add a selfie and a full-body photo.”
- **Action:** Click **Analyze & Style**.
- **Privacy:** Use only the photos approved for the public submission. Check the browser frame for filenames, notifications and personal tabs before recording.

### Shot 5 — The intelligence layer

- **Time:** 0:39–0:52
- **Visual:** Show “Curating your look.” Time-compress the processing sequence so several stages complete in 8–10 seconds. Never show a stalled spinner.
- **On-screen text:** `Measured by YouCam AI`
- **Voiceover:** “YouCam Skin Analysis and Skin Tone Analysis measure the colours and visible skin signals already present in your face, while Apparel VTO tries the strongest candidates on the same body.”
- **Edit:** Match cuts to processing-step completions. Keep the real step labels readable.

### Shot 6 — Palette reveal

- **Time:** 0:52–1:04
- **Visual:** Results appear. Hold on the colour-season title, measured feature swatches and recommended palette. Slowly pan or scroll through the section.
- **On-screen text:** `Measured. Explained. Never guessed.`
- **Voiceover:** “EventReady translates those measurements into a twelve-season colour profile and reveals the palette behind the decision.”
- **Optional microtext:** `12-season analysis • CIELAB • CIEDE2000`
- **Edit:** Let the music lift here. This is the first major reveal.

### Shot 7 — The proof

- **Time:** 1:04–1:17
- **Visual:** Fill the frame with the controlled side-by-side proof shot. Pause long enough to compare both garments. End on the displayed point gap.
- **On-screen text:** `Same person. Same pose. Only the colour changes.`
- **Voiceover:** “Then it proves the difference: the same person, the same pose and the same lighting. Only the garment colour changes.”
- **Edit:** Avoid a fast scroll. The viewer needs time to inspect both sides.

### Shot 8 — Signals become styling

- **Time:** 1:17–1:31
- **Visual:** Move from the skin reading to the hero recommendation. Land on the try-on image, match percentage and the three **Why it works** reasons.
- **On-screen text:** `Every point has a reason.`
- **Voiceover:** “Skin signals refine the fabric finish. Time of day changes how that finish is judged. And every recommendation carries a reason you can actually read.”
- **Edit:** Add three restrained highlights, one for each visible reason. Do not cover the real interface copy.

### Shot 9 — One answer

- **Time:** 1:31–1:43
- **Visual:** Hold on the recommended outfit. Push from the match score to the try-on image, then to colour palette, silhouette and fabric finish.
- **On-screen text:** `Your best match—not another grid.`
- **Voiceover:** “So instead of another endless grid, you get one best match, a confidence score, and a clear explanation of why it works for you.”

### Shot 10 — From verdict to action

- **Time:** 1:43–1:59
- **Visual:** Show the generated outfit motion clip for 4–5 seconds. Then cut to **Other strong matches** and continue to **Shop your palette**, where several real listing cards are visible.

### Shot 11 — Trust

- **Audio:** Resolve on the brand name. Hold the final frame for one full second after the voiceover ends.

## Editing Rules

1. **Show the working product, not a slide deck.** At least 80% of the video should be the real app or its real output.
2. **Do not call it a hackathon project, prototype or experiment.** Speak as if EventReady AI is already a product.
3. **Do not use Demo Mode in the recording.** The opening button and demo banner must never appear after the first screen.
4. **Edit out all waiting.** Compress processing and pre-generate the outfit motion clip.
5. **Keep the face consistent.** The controlled same-person comparison is a central proof point.
6. **Use captions throughout.** White or warm ivory text with a subtle dark backing; two lines maximum.
7. **Keep overlays short.** Never place a paragraph over the interface.
8. **Show the cursor only when it communicates a choice.** Hide it during result reveals and slow pans.
9. **Do not overclaim.** Say “confidence score,” “measures,” “recommends” and “shopping leads.” Do not claim medical analysis, perfect fit, guaranteed appearance or product-level measurement for retail listings.
10. **End before 2:30.** The required range is 1–3 minutes, and judges are not required to watch beyond three minutes.

## Capture Checklist

- [ ] Record at 1920×1080 in a clean browser window.
- [ ] Set browser zoom to 90–100%, then keep it unchanged for every clip.
- [ ] Close personal tabs, notifications, bookmarks and password-manager popups.
- [ ] Rehearse the exact Live Mode photos once before recording.
- [ ] Confirm the result includes colour analysis, proof shot, skin read, three try-ons, recommendation, shopping section and motion clip.
- [ ] Capture the complete live run once as safety footage.
- [ ] Record each results section again as a separate slow, clean clip.
- [ ] Record voiceover separately in a quiet room.
- [ ] Add burned-in captions and verify spelling against the app.
- [ ] Export H.264 MP4, 1080p, 15–25 Mbps.
- [ ] Watch the exported file once with sound and once muted.
- [ ] Upload publicly to YouTube before the submission deadline and wait for HD processing to finish.
